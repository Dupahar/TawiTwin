    For city corporations

    Tawi Twin is a monsoon and heat command layer for an Integrated Command & Control Centre: building-level risk, live alerts, where to send pumps, which drains to clear, and a citizen channel with AI triage. Every input is free global data, so a new city can be onboarded in a day.

    What changes, and for whom

    A resident in Gujjar Nagar asks in Hindi “kal paani bharega?” and gets a yes/no for the next 48 h, why, what to do, and the nearest hospital and high-ground school. One tap shares it on WhatsApp.
    The control room sees which areas and how many buildings are at risk before the rain, where to pre-position dewatering pumps, which drains to clear first, and citizen photos already triaged by AI.
    The planning wing gets a ranked pre-monsoon list of drains and areas, weighted by how many people live there, as a CSV.

    Does it match what happened?

    We checked 8 localities that news reports named as flooded on 26–27 Aug 2025 (inside the mapped area). Near them, 36% of buildings are rated high or very high flood exposure, against 25% city-wide (1.5×); for “very high” it is 2.9% vs 0.8%. Nikki Tawi, Beli Charan and Bhagwati Nagar are clear hits. Gurkha Nagar, Qasim Nagar and Rajiv Nagar are misses: the 30 m terrain model includes buildings, and the radar passed a day after the peak. Small sample: a sanity check, not an accuracy figure. Sources: Onmanorama, Sphere India SitRep, News On AIR.

    The event behind it

    On 26–27 Aug 2025 Jammu received a record 296 mm of rain in 24 hours, the Tawi crossed its highest flood level and part of the 4th Tawi Bridge at Bhagwati Nagar gave way.

    Evidence

    • Flood: Sentinel-1 radar, 27 Aug 2025 18:34 IST vs 15 Aug (change detection, OpenCV).
    • Terrain: Copernicus DEM 30 m, height above nearest drain.
    • Buildings: 188,180 footprints, Overture Maps (AWS Open Data). Heights are a uniform 6 m (not in source).
    • Heat: Landsat 8/9 surface temperature, Apr–Jun 2024–26.
    • Land change: Sentinel-2, 2019-20 vs 2025-26, in the flood-prone zone.
    • Forecast: Open-Meteo, refreshed hourly.

    Limits

    Radar misses water between buildings. The flood-prone zone is a proxy, not the official demarcation. Drains are those mapped in OpenStreetMap only. Land-cover change is detected change, not a finding of encroachment. Not an official warning: follow JMC, SDMA and IMD. Emergency: 112.

    Built on AWS

    CloudFront + S3 (site, vector tiles, photos) · API Gateway + Lambda on Graviton · DynamoDB · EventBridge Scheduler · Amazon Bedrock with Strands Agents (chat + photo triage) · Amazon Location Service · AWS Open Data. Deployed with AWS SAM. Map: Mapbox.
